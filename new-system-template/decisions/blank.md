# NNNN. The decision, in one short statement

Status: proposed, or accepted with the date, or replaced by the record that replaces it.

## Context

What made a decision necessary: the problem, the constraints, and what was known when it was made.

## Decision

What was decided, precisely enough that someone reading the code could tell whether it follows the decision. Name the rule card and the check, if the decision has them.

## Consequences

What the decision makes easier and what it makes harder. What it now misses, and who reviews that.
